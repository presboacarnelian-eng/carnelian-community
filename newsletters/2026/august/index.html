Architectural Review

Thinking about improving your home?
Check with ARC first.

If you’re changing something on the exterior of your home, especially something visible from the street or common area, check with management before beginning the work.

🖌️

Exterior Painting

New colors or changing your existing color scheme.

🪟

Windows

Replacement windows, frame colors, grids, tint and more.

🚪

Doors & Garage Doors

Entry doors, garage doors, security doors or changes in style or color.

🌿

Landscaping

Significant changes to visible landscaping, hardscape or permanent features.

💡

Exterior Lighting

New fixtures or changes to existing exterior lighting.

🏡

Patio & Structures

Patio covers, pergolas, awnings, fences, gates and more.

Not sure? Ask first.

A quick question before ordering materials or scheduling a contractor can prevent delays, unexpected costs, or having to make changes later.

The ARC process is simple

1

Plan

Decide what you’re changing and gather your project information.

2

Submit

Provide your request and supporting documents.

3

Review

The Association reviews your proposal.

4

Approval

Wait for the applicable approval before beginning work.

5

Improve!

Complete the work according to what was approved.
