Carnelian Community · October 2026

Community Newsletter

Seasonal maintenance, safety and shared responsibility — practical updates to help keep Carnelian clean, welcoming and well cared for.

As fall arrives, the Carnelian Community Board is sharing important updates on seasonal pool operations, responsible pet ownership, water conservation and preparing our homes for cooler, wetter weather. Thank you for doing your part to care for our neighborhood.

Important pool update

01. Pool Heater Shutdown — October 19

The community pool heater was turned off effective October 19, 2026, as part of seasonal operations. The pool remains open, although the water will gradually cool as temperatures decrease. This change applies to the pool heater only; it does not announce any change to spa heating. Please consider water temperature and weather conditions before swimming.

Community responsibility

02. Pet Waste: Please Pick Up Every Time

We have received increasing complaints about pet waste being left throughout our community. This needs immediate attention. Pet waste on sidewalks, lawns, landscaped areas and shared spaces creates unsanitary conditions and places an unfair burden on neighbors and landscaping personnel.

  • Carry waste bags whenever you walk your pet.
  • Pick up waste immediately, wherever it occurs.
  • Dispose of used bags in an appropriate trash receptacle; do not leave bags along sidewalks or in landscaped areas.
  • Respect neighbors and the community spaces everyone uses.
Leaving pet waste behind is not acceptable community behavior. Thank you to the many pet owners who consistently clean up after their animals.

Homeowner tips & reminders

03. Rainy Season & Sprinkler Management

As a follow-up to September’s reminder, please monitor your household irrigation as wetter weather approaches. Check forecasts and reduce or pause sprinklers when rainfall provides sufficient moisture; resume watering as needed during dry periods. Inspect sprinkler heads for leaks, overspray and runoff, and avoid watering saturated soil. Thoughtful irrigation helps conserve water, protect landscaping and reduce unnecessary utility costs. Report community-maintained irrigation problems to management rather than adjusting HOA equipment.

Homeowner tips & reminders

04. Sod & Landscaping Care

New sod needs time to root: minimize foot traffic and follow the installing landscaper’s watering instructions, which may differ from established lawn needs. Remove leaves and debris, avoid cutting grass too short, and watch for brown patches, standing water or soggy soil that may indicate irrigation or drainage issues. Please avoid walking through newly restored common-area landscaping and report damage or leaks to management.

Homeowner tips & reminders

05. Spiders & Ants: Prevention Around the Home

For spiders, remove accessible webs, reduce clutter around garages and exterior walls, seal gaps around doors and windows, and limit unnecessary lighting that attracts insects. For ants, promptly clean crumbs and spills, seal food, avoid leaving pet food outside, address moisture, and keep trash containers closed. Persistent ant activity may benefit from appropriately placed enclosed baits or professional assistance. Always follow pesticide labels and keep products away from children and pets. Report common-area concerns to management.

Homeowner tips & reminders

06. Storm Drain Preparedness

Help keep rainwater moving by removing leaves and loose debris from around your property, securing trash, and never sweeping grass clippings or soil into drains. Watch for standing water or blocked community drainage and report it promptly. Do not lift drain covers, enter drainage structures or attempt hazardous clearing during a storm.

Homeowner tips & reminders

07. Parking & Garage Courtesy

Please use your garage and driveway for household vehicles and help preserve guest parking for visitors. Avoid blocking driveways, sidewalks, gates or access routes; park within designated spaces and allow room for pedestrians and neighbors. Review the Association’s published parking rules for complete requirements. Safe emergency access and considerate parking benefit everyone.

Homeowner tips & reminders

08. Reporting Community Maintenance

Please report broken common-area sprinklers, landscaping damage, pool equipment concerns, malfunctioning gates, lighting problems, drainage issues and walkway hazards to Optimum Professional Property Management. Include the exact location, a short description and photographs if available. Contact Bryant Sanchez at (714) 508-9070, ext. 284 or bsanchez@optimumpm.com. For immediate threats to life or safety, contact emergency services.

Homeowner tips & reminders

09. Holiday Decorations: Safety & Courtesy

As holiday decorating begins, use outdoor-rated lighting and cords outside, inspect wiring, avoid overloaded outlets, and secure decorations against wind and rain. Keep sidewalks, driveways and access paths clear. Be considerate of neighbors when using bright or flashing lights, and consult applicable community guidance for exterior displays.

Homeowner tips & reminders

10. Exterior Lighting & Home Security

Shorter days are a good reminder to check porch and entry lighting, replace burned-out bulbs, inspect fixtures, and make sure doors, windows and gates close securely. Keep garage doors closed when not in use, avoid leaving valuables visible in vehicles, and report malfunctioning common-area lighting. Aim motion lighting to avoid unnecessary glare into neighboring homes.

Homeowner tips & reminders

11. ARC Approval: Plan Before You Begin

Before starting exterior improvements or certain landscaping changes, review the Association’s architectural requirements and determine whether an Architectural Review Committee (ARC) application is needed. Obtain required approval before beginning work. Visit the ARC resources and application page. Questions and applications may be directed to communication@optimumpm.com.
