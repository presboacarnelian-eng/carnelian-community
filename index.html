Community Reminder

Wednesday Street Cleaning

Help keep Carnelian clean and accessible.

Street cleaning takes place every Wednesday. Residents and guests are asked to move vehicles from the street so the street sweeper can properly service the community.

Vehicles left on the street can prevent areas from being cleaned and may result in portions of the community being missed.

Please move vehicles off the street before Wednesday street cleaning and remind household members, tenants and guests as well. Thank you for helping keep Carnelian clean and well maintained.
