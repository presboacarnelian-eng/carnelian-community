Treasurer's note

Steady, with a few items under review

The Association closed the reporting period in a generally strong position. Operating expenses remain below plan overall, reserve contributions are being made on schedule, and reserve funds continue to support long-term community needs such as gates, fencing, paving and pool-related projects.

The Board is continuing to watch utility costs and pool/spa service expenses while preparing the next budget cycle. Reserve reinvestment and the updated reserve study also remain active items.

What is not shown here: bank balances, account numbers, vendor pricing, homeowner balances, delinquency amounts or other detailed financial records.
