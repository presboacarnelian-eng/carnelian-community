How Carnelian Works

Board, management & homeowners

👥

Board of Directors

Homeowners elect the Board to establish Association policy, approve budgets and contracts, make governance decisions and provide oversight. Individual directors are not the day-to-day customer-service desk for owner accounts or routine requests.

🤝

Property Management

Management handles day-to-day owner support and Association administration: account and portal questions, key fobs/access matters, compliance communications, ARC routing, common-area service requests, records and coordination with vendors.

🏠

Homeowners & Household Members

Owners are responsible for keeping their contact information current, reviewing Association communications, following governing documents and using the management/owner portal for routine requests. Household members can use this page to understand where questions should go.

Architectural Review

Planning a home improvement?

Start here before work begins. Carnelian's Architectural Review Application states that ARC approval is required before approved work commences.

ARC process at a glance

1. Plan your project.   2. Complete the application.   3. Attach the required supporting documents.   4. Submit the complete package for review.   5. Wait for written approval before beginning applicable work.

What to include: the completed application plus a marked-up plot plan/site survey showing the proposed improvement, property lines/setbacks and supporting measurements, drawings, color photos, samples or other information needed to understand the exterior appearance and location of the project.

Project-specific information may also be required. The application includes guidance for exterior paint, fences, landscaping changes, construction projects, roof changes and other exterior work.
